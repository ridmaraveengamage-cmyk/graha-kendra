Your sky wheel

All designs

The wheel is your birth chart. Each slice is one area of life; the gold line marks your rising sign. Tap a slice or a planet.

Slices are whole-sign houses counted from your rising sign (lagna); planets sit at their sidereal degree (Lahiri ayanamsa). "Today's sky" shows where the planets are now, on the dashed outer ring. Readings follow classical Parashari rules. For guidance and reflection only.
